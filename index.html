Methodology, Assumptions & Simulation Limitations

SUMO TraCI Integration

The step trajectories, phase indices, and queue metrics are collected through TraCI APIs from SUMO. While SUMO models microscopic physics (Krauss car-following), real driver behavior includes stochastic anomalies.

Isolated Node Simplification

The simulation models an isolated single 4-way intersection without corridor coordination or adjacent platoon progression.

Non-Connection to Physical Hardware

All presented control loops and metrics are purely computational. No physical traffic controller cabinet or road detector hardware is driven by this software.
